This privacy policy outlines how our restaurant ecommerce Online Ordering Platform collects, uses, and protects the personal information of its users. This policy is in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Collection of Personal Information

We collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you place an order on our Online Ordering Platform. We also collect information about your device, location, and browsing behavior on our Online Ordering Platform. This information is collected through cookies and/or similar technologies.

 Use of Personal Information

We use your personal information to process your orders, communicate with you about your order, and to improve our Online Ordering Platform and services. We may also use your information to send you marketing communications, but only if you have given us permission to do so by the way of enabling and disabling the subscription or notifications.

 Sharing of Personal Information

We do not share your personal information with any third parties except for the purposes of processing your order, such as with payment processors and shipping companies. We may also share your information with law enforcement agencies if required by law.

 Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. We use secure technologies such as SSL encryption to ensure that your information is transmitted securely over the internet.

 Retention of Personal Information

We will retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information that we have collected. You also have the right to object to the processing of your personal information and to withdraw your consent to receive marketing communications. To exercise your rights, please contact us at the contact information provided below.

 Changes to this Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes to this policy by email or by posting a notice on our Online Ordering Platform.

 Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our handling of your personal information, please contact us at info.chikex@gmail.com.
