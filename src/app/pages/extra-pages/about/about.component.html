About Us

our company We’re passionate about fastfood in 20+ years

A new professional cookery brand in the UAE will debut in the middle of 2022 with the objective of becoming the region's most popular restaurant chain. The culinary service at this lavish fast food restaurant is aimed to combine contemporary and tradition. ChikeX aspires to serve delicious, high-quality meals at an accessible price. Its major goal is to 'FEED THEM MORE with opulent cuisine, EXCEPTIONALLY TASTY.

It's a Halal-certified international quick service restaurant chain. In an ultra-modern setting, it serves fresh, tasty, and beautiful meals made with high-quality herbs and spices hand-picked from across the world to produce unique sensations.

The visionaries' long-held dream has come true thanks to their dedication to high-quality and global practices. The first order of business is to provide service to all emirates in the UAE. Chikex, as anticipated by our customer base, offers a distinct and diverse menu inspired by global flavors: Astringent American, Aromatic Arabic, mellow mexi, can, Invigorating Indian, and Irresistible Italian.
